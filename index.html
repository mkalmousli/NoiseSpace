    Why ambient sound?

    White, pink and brown noise mask distracting background chatter so you can concentrate. Rain, ocean and fireplace sounds ease stress and help you wind down for sleep. The 10 Hz focus tone is a binaural beat – use headphones.

    Everything is generated live in your browser with the Web Audio API: no downloads, no tracking.
